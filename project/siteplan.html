Swimming World

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Swimming World

I chose the name "Swimming World" because it clearly represents a website dedicated to swimming. The name is simple, memorable, and reflects a place where visitors can learn about swimming techniques, safety, fitness benefits, and equipment.

Optional Domain: swimmingworld.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide educational information about swimming as a hobby and fitness activity. Visitors will learn about swimming styles, safety guidelines, training tips, health benefits, and recommended swimming equipment. The site will help both beginners and experienced swimmers improve their knowledge and skills.

Scenarios

  • What swimming stroke is easiest for beginners to learn?
  • What safety rules should I follow when swimming in a pool or open water?
  • What equipment do I need to start swimming?
  • How can swimming improve my physical fitness and overall health?

Color Schema

Deep Blue (#0077B6) - Used for headers, navigation, and headings.
Light Aqua (#CAF0F8) - Used for page backgrounds and content sections.
White (#FFFFFF) - Used for content cards and containers.

Dark Gray (#333333) will be used for body text and paragraph content.

Typography

Poppins - Used for headings and navigation.

Arial - Used for body text, paragraphs, and lists.
